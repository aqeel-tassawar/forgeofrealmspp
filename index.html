Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 May 2026

Forge of Realms does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. The data practices described below are handled entirely by third-party SDKs (Google AdMob and Google Play Billing) under their own privacy policies.

1. Information We Collect

The game itself stores only the following data locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences:

  • Your current wave, coins, and lives
  • Your weapon grid layout and upgrade levels
  • Your selected difficulty
  • Your high score
  • Whether you have purchased the "Remove Ads" upgrade
  • Sound and music preferences

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Forge of Realms uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use the following data to serve relevant ads:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / GAID)
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size)
  • IP address (used to infer approximate location)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

In regions covered by GDPR (European Economic Area and UK) or other applicable privacy laws, a consent dialogue will be presented before any personalised ads are shown. You may withdraw or update consent at any time by reinstalling the app.

For full details, see Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

All purchases (Remove Ads, coin packs) are processed entirely by the Google Play Store. We do not receive or store any payment card details. Google's handling of purchase data is governed by the Google Play Terms of Service .

4. Notifications

With your permission, Forge of Realms sends a daily reminder notification to encourage you to play. No data is transmitted to any server in connection with these notifications. You can disable them at any time in your device's app notification settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Forge of Realms is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

All locally stored game data is deleted when you uninstall the app. To request deletion of any data held by Google AdMob or Google Play, please refer to Google's data deletion tools at myaccount.google.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
